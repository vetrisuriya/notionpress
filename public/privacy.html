What we store

Your Notion user ID and name, the name of each Notion workspace you connect, and an access token that lets us read the pages you shared. Tokens are encrypted in our database. We also store your GitHub account name, the installation ID, and the settings of each sync (page ID, repository, branch, folder, schedule, status). We do not store your email address.

To skip unchanged files we keep a short fingerprint (hash) of each exported page and file. We do not keep the text of your pages after a sync finishes.

What we do with your pages

During a sync we read the Notion pages you chose, convert them to HTML or Markdown, and commit them to the GitHub repository you chose. Nothing else is done with your content, and we do not share it with anyone else.

Public repositories

If you pick a public repository, anyone on the internet can read what we commit. You are responsible for what you publish.

Removing your data

You can remove a sync at any time. Revoking access in Notion or uninstalling the GitHub app stops all syncs. To have your records deleted, visit vetrisuriya.in/contact (or use the “Delete everything” button on your dashboard). Files already committed to your repository stay there until you delete them.
